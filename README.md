## 🎯 Purpose

AskJamie™ is not just a résumé—it's a clone of your best self. This repository powers an always-on conversational sales engine that:

- **Presents** your professional background naturally and authentically
- **Showcases** portfolio projects with context and impact
- **Engages** potential clients and collaborators effectively
- **Guides** conversations toward meaningful outcomes

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Knowledge Base**: Add your professional background and expertise to `/src/corpus/`
2. **Prompts**: Define your conversational style and engagement strategy in `/prompts/`
3. **Evaluation**: Create test scenarios in `/eval/` to ensure quality
4. **Build**: Compile packs using the seed templates in `/build/`
5. **Deploy**: Upload to your custom GPT configuration
